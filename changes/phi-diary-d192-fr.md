## The triad closes, and it closes in French too
These three lines close a sequence opened on day 190 and continued on day 191 — the alarm, then the silence, now the instrument attached to nothing:
- `Un instrument qui parle et que personne n'écoute.`
- `Un instrument qui a cessé de parler et que personne ne remarque.`
- `Et le troisième : un instrument en parfait état, juste en chacune de ses pièces, certifié, et qui n'est attaché à rien.`
The first two are isometric; the third keeps the relative and widens, as the English does. ## Title
`Le tiroir aux serrures` rather than *des serrures*: in French `aux` reads as a drawer kept FOR locks, like *une boîte aux lettres* — a container defined by what it holds, with nothing implying the locks are fitted to anything. That is the image the entry is built on.
