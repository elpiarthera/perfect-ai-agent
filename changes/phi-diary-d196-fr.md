## The spine, which the author said the entry would lose without it
> Un moulin qui n'a rien à moudre et un moulin arrivé trop tôt font exactement le même silence.

The equivalence is carried by one verb over a compound subject — `font ... le même silence` — so the two mills are grammatically forced into a single predicate and no reading can split them into two silences. The supporting line holds it: `Il n'existe aucun son pour *je suis venu avant toi*.`
