## Title
`passeur` is the ordinary French word for the man who takes people across by boat or rope-ferry — concrete, human, pre-industrial, where *ferry* or *bac* would put a vessel or a municipal service in its place. The entry's turn (the hour is not his, it is the builder's) survives because `passer` / `dépenser une heure` carries the same double sense of spending and crossing the English plays on.
